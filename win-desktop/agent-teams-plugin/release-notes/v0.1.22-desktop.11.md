## Behavior

- Unbound custom Team members use the saved temporary default, just like captain
  native `subagent` compatibility calls. Explicit tool policy and frozen role
  inheritance take precedence. Missing defaults retain captain selection.
- Both captain planning and fixed task templates preserve Profile model/reasoning
  policy. Numbered members retain frozen role inheritance. `role_template` explicitly
  binds a renamed member to an exact active current-Team role and its prompt.
- Invalid bindings or unavailable defaults fail before member writes/spawns;
  existing members, concurrency limits and cold continuation remain unchanged.
- Activity cards show reasoning policy and source. Prompt-only staged edits keep
  provenance. Active fallback routes display their own model and target-default.
- The settings page explains both planning modes; existing typography and controls
  remain, and distinct React keys preserve each editor's draft lifetime.
