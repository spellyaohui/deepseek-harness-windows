# @nanmicoder/dsh-agent-teams v0.1.15-desktop.4

## Changes

- Numbered members inherit the frozen unnumbered role template from the current Team, including `reviewer2` through higher numbered reviewers and the corresponding analyst, implementer, and tester variants.
- Supports hyphen, underscore, and space separators before positive numeric suffixes.
- Explicit Provider/model/reasoning settings remain authoritative; unmatched custom roles keep the captain route and ambiguous role-description matches fail closed.
- Omitting `reasoning_mode` at the model-facing add-member boundary no longer becomes an implicit `target-default`, so numbered roles keep inheriting their frozen base-role model policy.
- Added pure policy and real `agent_teams_add_member` lifecycle regressions for route, reasoning, precedence, and high-numbered role families.
